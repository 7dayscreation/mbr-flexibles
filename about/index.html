ABOUT US
-

Today, MBR group stands tall as a 50 years old renowned business entity. Our Group is proud of its rich legacy, which started its operations way back in 1960s. In 1960 MBR group was engaged in businesses of Chemicals, textile auxiliaries and bulk intermediates. MBR 'S identity as a group stems from its exhaustive experience and expertise of people. Its roots have been nurtured with an association of world class multinational and indigenous companies. It's been one of the largest business houses of specialty chemicals in India. MBR FLEXIBLES LTD is a Limited Company promoted by well-experienced promoters of MBR Group. The company has ventured into plastic stream with the main objective to enter into business of manufacturing activities of flexible packaging. MBR Group has served various markets for more than 56 years. Locally, we’ve been serving since 1960 with Chemicals, textile auxiliaries and bulk intermediates.
