QUALITY & SUSTAINABILITY
MBR Flexibles Ltd.

MBR respects the environment and is committed to environmentally sound business practices, this taking into account the need to preserve natural resources and save energy.

From an environmental perspective, MBR flexible provides the best product to package ratio of all the packaging options using less energy and less plastic, creating less waste and a smaller carbon footprint. We are highly inclined towards adequate waste management.

Our plant is designed to meet the international standards in cleanliness and hygiene, our plant is equipped to even meet the stringent norms for safety, health and environment stipulated by the Food and Pharmacy industries. This ensures that production is carried out in a dust and insect free environment. Additionally, a stringent Pest Control System makes the units absolutely pests free. With the above being achieved and practiced, our plant is certified by HACCP - ISO 9001 and other certificates.
