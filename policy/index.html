PRIVACY POLICY
MBR Flexibles Ltd.
1. OVERVIEW & COMMITMENT

At MBR Flexibles Ltd., we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us through our website. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning data collection, usage, and protection.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when submitting business inquiries, requesting packaging quotes, or communicating with us. This may include your name, corporate email address, contact telephone number, company name, and specific packaging requirements.

3. USE OF INFORMATION

Information submitted to MBR Flexibles Ltd. is used exclusively to respond to your queries, provide custom packaging solutions and price estimates, manage client accounts, and fulfill statutory obligations. We never sell, lease, or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect online against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

5. COOKIES & ANALYTICS

Our website may utilize standard analytical tools (such as Google Analytics) to aggregate anonymous traffic statistics, helping us improve site navigation and performance. These tools do not gather personally identifiable information.

6. CONTACT FOR PRIVACY CONCERNS

For any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please write to our privacy officer at info@mbrflexibles.com.
